An AI-agent pipeline that ports CUDA repositories to SYCL and only accepts a port when a hidden check confirms it
matches the original. It ported llm.c's bf16 GPT-2 trainer, which passes llm.c's own test on CPU, NVIDIA and AMD
GPUs, and llama2.cu, with byte-identical output on all three. Every result links to committed evidence.
